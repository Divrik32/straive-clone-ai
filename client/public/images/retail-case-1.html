Our Success Stories

Labour Optimization for a fashion retailer

We observed 25%+ reduction in overtime costs, 33% reduction in customer wait times and queue lengths along with a 40% reduction in scheduling-related grievances.

Market mix optimization for a grocery chain

Delivered a 25% increase in marketing ROI by developing web based scenario planning tools to enable effective budget allocation & granular models for 10+ channels & 5+ markets.

Real-time price intel for a Brazilian food retailer

Offered better pricing & discount strategies with our interactive dashboards, monitoring 500K competitor prices daily & enabling over 95% accuracy in product matching and classification.

Supply chain optimization for beverage giant

Deployed interactive visualizations for hotspot detection, leading to ~50% reduction in stock overage and improving transportation planning from vendor to destination.

Planogram compliance for global retailer

Developed a user-friendly mobile app to capture & verify shelf layouts, resulting in 30% improvement in planogram compliance and a 60% reduction in manual audit time & cost.

Food wastage reduction for fast-food chain

Reduced food waste by 28% in two months & increased app-based conversions by 15–18% through smart offer targeting, driving higher margins on perishable SKUs.
